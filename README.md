# Grey's Anatomy
**Grey's Anatomy** is one of my favorite medical drama series.  
It follows the lives of doctors at **Grey Sloan Memorial Hospital** as they navigate through their careers, friendships, and personal challenges. I love medical drama.

## Summary
The story begins with **Meredith Grey**, a surgical intern who dreams of becoming a great surgeon like her mother.  
Throughout the series, we see her and her colleagues grow from interns to skilled doctors while facing love, loss, and life-or-death moments in the hospital.
